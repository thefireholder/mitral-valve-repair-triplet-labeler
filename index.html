    Labels are stored as frame numbers (0-indexed, IN and OUT inclusive) with times derived from them, one Google Sheet tab per video. Every change is autosaved, and a copy is kept in this browser until the sheet confirms it.

    Setup

    1 Google Sheet

    Paste the Web app URL and the secret from the Apps Script deployment (see the README). They are kept only in this browser.

    2 Video folder

    Choose the folder that contains the patient folders (for example full_raw_data). Always choose the same folder, because each video is identified by its path inside it. Videos play from this computer and are never uploaded.
